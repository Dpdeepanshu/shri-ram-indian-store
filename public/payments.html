Payment methods

Choose how you pay

Cash on DeliveryPay in cash to the delivery person when your parcel arrives. Please keep the exact amount ready.
UPIPay with Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or any UPI app. The UPI ID is shared on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.
Bank transferPay by NEFT or IMPS to our bank account. Account details are shared on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.

How payment works

  • Place your order from the cart. It opens in WhatsApp with your chosen payment method.
  • We confirm stock and the total, then share payment details if you chose UPI or bank transfer.
  • For UPI or bank transfer, send the payment screenshot or reference number on WhatsApp. We dispatch after the payment is received.
  • All prices are in Indian rupees (₹) and include applicable GST.

Stay safe

  • We never ask for your card number, PIN, CVV or OTP.
  • Pay only to the UPI ID or bank account shown on this website or shared by us on our WhatsApp number.

Refunds

Approved refunds are returned to your UPI ID or bank account within 5 to 7 working days. See Shipping & Returns.
