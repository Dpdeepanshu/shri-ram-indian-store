Shipping & Returns

Shipping

  • Hum poore India me deliver karte hain.
  • Delivery me aam taur par lagte hain (location ke hisaab se kam/zyada ho sakte hain).
  • se upar ke orders par shipping free hai. Usse kam par shipping charge lagega.
  • Order confirm hone ke baad dispatch ki jaankari WhatsApp par di jaayegi.

Order kaise karein

Cart se order karne par aapki order detail WhatsApp par hamare paas aati hai. Hum aapse confirm karke payment (Cash on Delivery ya UPI/Bank transfer) aur delivery ki jaankari share karte hain.

Return & Exchange

  • Saamaan milne ke din ke andar return/exchange ke liye humse sampark karein.
  • Damaged, galat ya defective product aane par photo/video ke saath batayein, hum replace ya refund karenge.
  • Product unused ho, tags aur original packing ke saath ho.
  • Hygiene ke kaaran khole hue beauty products aur pehne hue/ use kiye hue jewellery aur kapde return nahi hote.

Refund

Approved return ke baad refund original payment tareeke se ya UPI se 5 se 7 working days me process hota hai.

Sampark

Kisi bhi madad ke liye par email karein ya par call/WhatsApp karein.
