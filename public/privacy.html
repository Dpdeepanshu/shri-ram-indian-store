Privacy policy

Information we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, mobile number, delivery address and order details so that we can process and deliver it.

How we use it

  • To confirm, deliver and support your order.
  • To contact you about your order by WhatsApp, phone or email.

We do not sell your information. We share only what is needed with our courier partners to deliver your order.

Third-party services

Orders are sent through WhatsApp, and this website is hosted on Cloudflare. Payments by UPI or bank transfer are handled by your own payment app or bank. These services have their own privacy policies. We do not store your card, PIN or OTP details.

Cookies and local storage

Your cart is saved in your browser (local storage). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.
